# Forge Tales — Privacy Policy


**Effective date:** 12 October 2026
**Contact:** The developer contact details on the game's Google Play page

## What this policy covers

This policy explains what information the mobile game **Forge Tales** ("the game") handles, why, and
what choices you have. The game has no accounts and no server of its own.

## Information the game handles

**Game progress.** Your progress (heroes, gear, stages, purchases made, settings) is saved in a file
on your device. It is not sent to us. If you delete the game or use *Settings → Delete my data*, it is
erased and cannot be recovered.

**Connection checks.** The game needs an internet connection. To check that there is one, it sends an
empty request to well-known public addresses operated by Google, Cloudflare and Apple. These requests
carry nothing about you beyond what any internet request carries (such as your IP address), and we do
not receive them.

**Purchases.** In-app purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see your payment details. Play
tells the game which item you bought so it can be delivered, and keeps a record of items that last
(No Ads, hero packs) so they can be restored on a new install. Unity IAP, the library that talks to
Google Play, may send purchase telemetry to Unity Technologies.

**Rewarded ads (Unity Ads).** The game offers optional ads that you choose to watch for a reward. They
are served by Unity Ads, whose SDK collects device information such as the advertising identifier, IP
address (approximate location), device model, diagnostics and how you interact with the ads, in order
to show the ad, pay for it and prevent fraud. **The game asks Unity Ads for non-personalised ads
only**: it does not use your data to target ads at you. If you buy *No Ads*, no ads are shown at all.
Unity's privacy policy: https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

**Notifications.** If you allow notifications, the game schedules reminders on your device (for
example when your hammers are full). They are created on the device; nothing is sent to us. You can
turn them off in Settings or in your device settings.

## What we do not do

We do not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos, microphone or precise location. We do
not run analytics of our own, and we do not sell or share personal information.

## Children

The game is meant for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly
collect personal information from children.

## Your choices and rights

* Delete your game data at any time: *Settings → Delete my data*.
* Reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.
* Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, the UK, California), you may have the right to
  access, correct or delete personal information and to object to certain processing. We hold no game
  data about you; for data held by Unity, use the contact in Unity's policy above; for anything else,
  use the developer contact details on the game's Google Play page.

## Changes

We may update this policy. The new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date
before it applies.
